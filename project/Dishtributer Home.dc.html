One tool for the parts of service that happen before service

Built around what restaurants already do on the phone and in notebooks — bookings, deposits, set menus, partner referrals and review chasing.

01

Pre-order links

Create a booking, pick which menus apply, and share one link. Guests order and pay in a couple of taps.

02

À la carte and set menus

Two-course, three-course or full à la carte, with sub-item choices per course and per guest.

03

Kitchen prep exports

A CSV counting every dish for the pass, and a second one listing guests for the door.

04

Referral partners

Give hotels, taxi firms and concierges a card and a link. Every booking they send is attributed and rewarded.

05

Reviews after the plate

Ask for the review when the meal is still fresh, on the number that made the booking.

06

Stripe underneath

Payment links, refunds and payouts run on your own Stripe account. No float, no middleman.

Guests pay up front

Print the prep list
